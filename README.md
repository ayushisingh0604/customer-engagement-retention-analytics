# Customer Engagement and Project Utilisation Analytics for Retention Strategy

## Project Overview
A Financial Analyst live project completed under **Unified Mentor**, focused on understanding customer churn patterns, customer engagement, product utilisation, and relationship indicators in a banking dataset.

The project uses Microsoft Excel and Power BI to analyse customer behaviour, identify segments associated with higher churn, and develop data-driven retention recommendations.

## Project Objectives
- Analyse customer retention and churn patterns.
- Explore customer engagement and activity levels.
- Evaluate churn across demographic and geographic segments.
- Examine product utilisation and relationship tiers.
- Identify customer segments associated with higher attrition.
- Develop business recommendations to support retention strategy.

## Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data validation, cleaning, feature engineering, PivotTables, exploratory analysis and charts.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** KPI cards, interactive dashboard, charts and slicers.

## Dataset
- **Records:** 10,000 customers
- **Original variables:** 14 columns
- **Industry:** Banking & Financial Services
- **Dataset:** European Bank Customer Dataset

Data preparation included checking missing values, duplicates and CustomerId uniqueness, validating binary variables, and creating derived analytical columns such as AgeGroup, EngagementStatus, ProductCategory, RelationshipScore and RelationshipTier.

| KPI | Result |

## Key Findings

- **Geography:** Germany showed a higher churn rate (32.44%) than France (16.15%) and Spain (16.67%).
- **Age:** Customers aged 45–54 and 55–64 had particularly high churn rates.
- **Engagement:** Inactive customers showed higher churn (26.85%) than active customers (14.27%).
- **Product utilisation:** Customers holding three and four products showed very high churn rates, although these groups were small.
- **Relationship tiers:** The custom Weak relationship tier had higher churn (28.17%) than Moderate (19.87%) and Strong (11.80%).

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate customer churn patterns in Germany and design targeted retention initiatives.
- Re-engage inactive customers through appropriate engagement programmes.
- Prioritise retention attention for customers in higher-churn age segments.
- Review product suitability and customer experience for multi-product customers, especially small high-churn groups.
- Strengthen customer relationships and monitor engagement and retention KPIs regularly.

## Project Deliverables
- Excel data preparation and analysis workbook.
- Exploratory analysis and supporting charts.
- Power BI customer churn dashboard.
- Business insights and retention recommendations.
- Detailed project report and PDF documentation.

## Limitations
The dataset does not include actual revenue, profit, retention costs, acquisition costs or Customer Lifetime Value (CLV). Therefore, this project identifies churn patterns and business opportunities but does not quantify financial impact. The relationship score and tier are custom analytical indicators, and observed associations do not establish causation.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates a data-driven approach to customer engagement and churn analysis using Excel and Power BI. It translates customer patterns into practical retention recommendations and identifies areas for further financial analysis when additional data becomes available.
